# Technical & Licensing Landscape for a Western Canada Trucking / Oilfield Logistics Navigation App (LeaseOS / FieldRoute)

## TL;DR
- **The core architecture is viable but legally constrained.** You can build LeaseOS by combining free/open township-grid data (Alberta ATS v4.1 and BC grids are free; Saskatchewan SaskGrid is paid), a commercial truck-routing API (HERE Routing v8 is the best value and the only mainstream API that bills truck routing at the same rate as a car route), and offline vector maps/routing (Mapbox Navigation SDK or self-hosted Valhalla). **The single biggest legal trap is that both Google Maps Platform and Apple MapKit forbid using their Places/POI data with a non-Google/non-Apple map** — so you cannot legally use Google Places search while rendering a Mapbox map and routing with HERE. Use HERE Places, Mapbox Search, Foursquare, or Overture/OSM instead.
- **The market is already crowded.** "Lease Locator" (Wolf Mask, Alberta, 2018), OilTrails, and GeoActivity Well Finder all do LSD→map→navigate, and Petro Ninja (now StackDX Maps) dominates O&G mapping. Your differentiation must be genuine truck/TDG routing, which none of these do well.
- **DLS conversion must use official "frozen" coordinate/polygon files, not a formula** — road allowances, correction lines, and township irregularities make algorithmic conversion drift by tens to hundreds of metres. Alberta's own regulator warns its algorithmic tool "approximates LSDs without considering road allowances."

## Key Findings

### 1. "Lease Locator" and comparable oilfield location apps
There is a specific app literally named **Lease Locator** (developer Wolf Mask, registered as 2060119 Alberta Ltd., Suite 518, 1811 4 St SW, Calgary AB T2S 1W2; also active in Fort St. John BC; first released 2018), on both Google Play (`com.terraform.lsdlocate`) and the Apple App Store. It converts LSD/NTS/UWI/UTM/lat-long to a map pin, gives Google-based turn-by-turn directions, [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.terraform.lsdlocate&hl=en_CA) and offers an **offline database described in its App Store listing as "Over 18.7 Million LSD's that will work when you are out of service area"** behind a premium subscription. Phone support: (403) 805-0104.

Direct competitors in the "LSD-to-wellsite navigation" niche:
- **OilTrails** (oiltrails.ca) — started 2012 for Alberta oilpatch workers; offline LSD + NTS lookups, a database of over 3 million well records, 3D satellite mapping, multiple navigation providers, and a browser companion. [OilTrails](https://oiltrails.ca/)
- **GeoActivity Well Finder** — Canada + US (13 US states), LSD/NTS/PLSS search, turn-by-turn navigation, well metadata (operator, depth, drill date). [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.geoactivity.finder&hl=en_US)
- **Petro Ninja / StackDX Maps** (Petro Niche Technology Ltd., Calgary) — the dominant free O&G mapping web + mobile platform for Western Canada; [Petroninja](https://help.petroninja.com/) wells, pipelines, facilities, mineral rights, surface dispositions; [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/stackdx-maps/id1208288818) premium tiers for connected datasets. Not primarily a turn-by-turn navigation product.

**Takeaway:** The LSD-finder + consumer navigation space is saturated and largely free. None of these products offer true **commercial-truck / dangerous-goods routing** — that is the defensible gap for LeaseOS/FieldRoute.

### 2. DLS / ATS spatial data — sources, licensing, cost
**Alberta (ATS v4.1) — FREE.** The authoritative product is the **Alberta Township System (ATS) V4.1 Polygons** (LSD, quarter section, section each *with statutory road allowances*, plus townships), derived from the master ATS coordinate file "frozen" by the Government of Alberta on March 31, 2005. Distributed by **AltaLIS** (altalis.com) and Alberta Open Data (open.alberta.ca/opendata/property-alberta-township-system-ats) under the **Open Government Licence – Alberta at no cost**. REST service: `https://geospatial.alberta.ca/titan/rest/services/base/alberta_township_system/MapServer`. Custodian: Alberta Data Partnerships Ltd. (formerly Spatial Data Warehouse). [Open Government Portal](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/76874ae6-373f-4371-bc99-e319a4fb84a3?wbdisable=false)

**British Columbia — FREE.** BC PNG tenure uses the NTS-like Petroleum & Natural Gas Grid, **except the Peace River Block, which uses the DLS Township/Range system on the 6th Meridian**. Sources, all under the **Open Government Licence – British Columbia**:
- **BC Energy Regulator (BCER) Open Data Portal** (data-bc-er.opendata.arcgis.com) — "Township Range DLS GRID" and "Quarter Section DLS GRID" datasets (custodian: Surveyor General Division, [Arcgis](https://data-bc-er.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/697e466746c74549aedeaca00671f031_7/about) LTSA); REST: `https://geoweb-ags.bcogc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/REFERENCE/GRID_EXT/MapServer`.
- **BC Data Catalogue** (catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca) — "Petroleum and Natural Gas Grid" plus BCGS 1:20,000 / 1:10,000 / 1:5,000 / 1:2,500 grids and NTS 250K grid (custodian GeoBC), available via custom download, KML, WMS/WFS.

**Saskatchewan — PAID.** The **ISC SaskGrid Township Fabric Map** (Information Services Corporation, isc.ca) provides township/section/quarter-section/LSD/monument layers approximating the DLS grid [Saskatchewan](https://gis.saskatchewan.ca/arcgis/rest/services/Cadastre/MapServer) to LSD level province-wide (surveyed in the south, theoretic interpolation in the north; each feature tagged "Surveyed" or "Theoretic"). [saskatchewan](https://gisappl.saskatchewan.ca/Geocortex/Essentials/EXT/Rest/sites/GeoPlanner/map?f=pjson) Free web view-only services exist at `gis.saskatchewan.ca`, but the downloadable dataset is a paid annual subscription. The last publicly documented price (ISC "Maps-Photos-Fees-Table-07-2017.pdf") is **$202.91/year for provincial coverage, or $304.36/year with the administrative boundary overlay**; current 2026 pricing is "contact us." Full Sask surface cadastral (parcel) data is far more expensive (~$52,755/yr provincial).

**Manitoba** also uses the DLS; the province distributes township/parcel data through its land-information services — verify licensing directly if you expand east (not confirmed in this research).

**Open-source DLS conversion — limited, and none purely algorithmic is reliable.** Confirmed repos: `floglass/ATS_LSD_Convert` (Python) — but notably it uses a **lookup table built from AltaLIS ATS polygon centroids**, not a formula; and a **Racine Ennis .NET library** covering DLS and BC NTS. No robust, well-maintained PyPI/npm package surfaced. Hosted tools/APIs include Township Canada (API, plans from ~$10/mo), [Township Canada](https://www.townshipcanada.com/pricing) Grid Atlas / LSD Finder, and legallandconverter.com (GeoJSON API).

**Known accuracy problems with algorithmic (non-shapefile) DLS conversion:**
- **Correction lines / range jogs**: because a square grid is imposed on a curved Earth, range numbering is reset along baselines every 4 townships, producing "jogs" that grow the farther west of a meridian you are. [Racine Ennis](https://racineennis.ca/2018/08/31/Nts-grid-coordinate-system)
- **Road allowances**: statutory road allowances (1 chain / 66 ft ≈ 20 m in Alberta's Third System; 1½ chains / ~30 m in older townships) are *added* to township size, so base lines are not exactly 24 miles apart and sections are offset.
- **Township irregularities**: townships are quadrilaterals, not perfect squares — the northern boundary is shorter than the southern, and closings on correction lines absorb accumulated error. [Kalmakov](http://www.kalmakov.com/historical/the%20dominion%20land%20survey.html)
- Consequently, the DLS is **survey-based, so there can be gaps in coverage** [Racine Ennis](https://racineennis.ca/2018/08/31/Nts-grid-coordinate-system) , and a pure formula drifts by tens to hundreds of metres, worst near correction lines. The Alberta Energy Regulator's own conversion tool explicitly warns that "Alberta Township System LSDs are approximated without considering road allowances." [Aer](https://www1.aer.ca/GISConversionTools/conversion_tools.html) This is exactly why Alberta distributes a frozen ATS v4.1 coordinate file rather than a formula. **Recommendation: snap to official polygon/coordinate files (ATS v4.1, SaskGrid, BCER DLS grid), never compute from a formula.**

### 3. Commercial vehicle & dangerous-goods (TDG) routing APIs
- **HERE Routing API v8** — strong truck profile: transport mode `truck`, physical restrictions (weight, weightPerAxle/weightPerAxleGroup, height, width, length), legal restrictions, and hazmat via `shippedHazardousGoods` (e.g., explosive, flammable, harmfulToWater). Roads prohibited for dangerous goods are excluded from route calculation. [HERE Developer](https://developer.here.com/documentation/routing-api/8.8.0/dev_guide/topics/use-cases/truck-routing.html) HERE Maps for Canada carries up to 900 data attributes including HERE Trucks / road-infrastructure layers. [Europa](https://www.europa.uk.com/map-data/here-maps/country/?cc=ca) **Pricing**: freemium ~250,000 transactions/month free, then about $1 per 1,000; a Pro plan around $449/month for 1M transactions. [APIScout](https://apiscout.dev/guides/google-maps-vs-here-maps-api-2026) Critically, per HERE partner Placematic, "**Truck routing draws from the same 5,000/month allowance and bills at the same rate as a car route — the vehicle profile does not move you to a different meter**." HERE increased Base Plan and pay-per-transaction list pricing by ~6% from April 1, 2026 for new contracts and renewals.
- **Trimble Maps / PC*MILER** — the North American freight industry standard; truck attributes, hazmat attribution, toll data, and a commercial-vehicle road network (restrictions, control zones, access rules). RESTful Web Services APIs; PC*MILER Web from about $84.99/month per user, API/enterprise by quote. Country-by-country data tiers (Navigable / Intermediate / Entry) — verify the Canada rural tier for your operating area.
- **PTV Developer** — enterprise European-origin routing with truck profiles and hazmat; strong in Europe; verify Western Canada rural coverage before committing.
- **Google Maps Platform — Large Vehicle Routing (LVR)**: announced general availability on August 17, 2026 (blog by PM Mohit Moondra) across Routes API, Route Optimization API, and Navigation SDK. [Google Maps Platform](https://mapsplatform.google.com/resources/blog/introducing-large-vehicle-routing-address-the-unique-logistics-needs-of-large-vehicle-fleets/) Per Google's Routes API docs, LVR is available only in "**the contiguous 48 United States (Generally Available) and Japan (Experimental). It isn't available in Alaska, Hawaii, or US territories**." It supports height/weight/length/width/axle count and hazmat classes, returns a route token for the Navigation SDK, and flags `routeRestrictionsPartiallyIgnored` when it can't fully comply. **Because it excludes Canada, it is not yet usable for Alberta/Saskatchewan/BC.** Standard Google Maps/Waze have no truck mode at all. [Argus AI](https://www.getargus.ai/company/blog/is-there-a-google-maps-for-trucks)
- **Mapbox** — Directions API profiles are driving, driving-traffic, walking, cycling (plus EV routing); [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/api/navigation/directions/) **no dedicated truck/hazmat profile**. Not a truck-routing solution out of the box.
- **Azure Maps** — Route Directions API supports a `truck` travel mode with truck attributes (width, height, weight, turning radius, cargo type incl. hazmat/flammable restrictions), but truck routing is only available in specific countries per its coverage tables [Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/azure-maps/routing-coverage) — **verify Canada is in the truck-routing coverage list before relying on it**.

**Canadian truck-restriction data reality**: HERE and Trimble are the two with genuine commercial-vehicle attribution (bridge heights, weight limits, hazmat restrictions) and North American coverage. Even so, **no commercial API reliably encodes Alberta/Saskatchewan seasonal road bans or municipal dangerous-goods routes** — these change dynamically and by bylaw, so you must overlay government feeds yourself (Section 6). Rural "lease road / ice road / goat trail" coverage is weak in every consumer and most commercial datasets — which is why incumbents (OilTrails, Lease Locator) lean on satellite imagery and user-added pins.

### 4. Google Maps Platform & Apple MapKit ToS constraints (critical)
**Google Maps Platform** Service-Specific Terms contain, for essentially every data API, a "**No use with a non-Google map**" clause. Verbatim (§5.3 / §14.2): "*Customer must not use Google Maps Content from the Places API in conjunction with a non-Google map*" [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms) — with identical clauses for Directions, Distance Matrix, Geocoding, [google](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms/index-20240422) Geolocation, and Roads APIs. Caching is limited: latitude/longitude may be **temporarily cached for up to 30 consecutive days**, then deleted; [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/maps-platform/terms/maps-service-terms) Place IDs may be stored per policy. **Practical conclusion: you CANNOT legally use Google Places to search POIs while rendering a Mapbox map and routing with HERE.** One narrow, current exception: the newer **Places UI Kit** (§15.1) — "*Customer may use Places UI Kit in Customer Applications with or without any map, including a non-Google Map. This clause will prevail over the No Use with Non-Google Maps clause*" — and Geocoding/Distance Matrix/Places may be used with *no* map at all, but not alongside a competing visible map.

**Apple MapKit / MapKit JS** is even more restrictive: Map Data "**may be cached, pre-fetched, or stored... only on a temporary and limited basis**"; [lawinsider](https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/additional-restrictions/_2) you may not build a secondary/derived database, and Map Data "will be displayed only on an Apple map" [lawinsider](https://www.lawinsider.com/clause/additional-restrictions/_2) — you cannot surface Apple Maps results on a non-Apple map. Additionally, **MapKit JS "may not be used... for the following commercial purposes: fleet management (including dispatch), asset tracking, enterprise route optimization"** [lawinsider](https://www.lawinsider.com/dictionary/mapkit-js) — which likely disqualifies MapKit JS for a commercial oilfield-dispatch product outright.

**Compliant POI alternatives** (usable with any map/routing engine):
- **HERE Places / Geocoding & Search** — 120M+ POIs, [SafeGraph](https://www.safegraph.com/guides/google-places-api-alternatives/) integrates with HERE routing; standard plans restrict permanent storage (enterprise licence for caching/export). [Mapscaping](https://mapscaping.com/guide-to-geocoding-api-pricing/)
- **Mapbox Search Box API** — POI/address search; first 100,000 requests free, then ~$0.75 per 1,000; [Medium](https://medium.com/@paulotaylor/google-places-api-alternatives-a-guide-to-save-you-big-money-with-genai-774605e04769) designed to pair with Mapbox maps.
- **Foursquare Places API** — 100M+ POIs, 200+ countries. Note: Foursquare **deprecated its legacy V3 endpoints on May 15, 2026** and moved to new rates on **June 1, 2026 — ~$15 per 1,000 Pro calls after 500 free/month, dropping to $12/1,000 above 100,000 calls and $9/1,000 above 500,000**; strict caching/attribution rules.
- **Overture Maps / OpenStreetMap + Overpass** — free, self-hostable, cacheable/storable; lower POI density in rural Western Canada but no derived-data restrictions.

### 5. Offline mapping & routing for remote areas
- **Mapbox** — Maps SDK offline (download tile regions/style packs; default ceiling 6,000 tiles per user, adjustable; [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/ios/legacy/maps/guides/offline/) tile-pack limit 750) [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/ios/maps/examples/offline-manager/) **plus** Navigation SDK offline via TileStore, which copies routing data on-device for **offline route calculation and rerouting with no connectivity** [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/ios/navigation/v2/guides/advanced/offline/) — the most turnkey commercial option. Mapbox ToS forbids redistributing downloaded offline maps, [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/ios/legacy/maps/guides/offline/) and Mapbox has no truck profile.
- **MapLibre** — open-source renderer (fork of Mapbox GL); pairs with offline vector tiles (MBTiles/PMTiles); rendering only, no routing.
- **Valhalla** — open-source, tile-based, with a native **truck costing model** that reads maxweight/maxheight/hazmat tags and exposes them as per-request parameters; can run on-device/offline; North America tiles ~15–20 GB. [SumGuy's Ramblings](https://sumguy.com/osrm-vs-valhalla-vs-graphhopper/) **Best open-source default for offline truck routing.**
- **OSRM** — fastest but least flexible; truck rules must be hand-encoded in Lua profiles; [Geospatialrouting](https://www.geospatialrouting.com/routing-api-automation-fleet-integration/comparing-routing-engines-for-production/) no native isochrones. [Pi Stack](https://www.pistack.xyz/posts/2026-04-25-graphhopper-vs-osrm-vs-valhalla-self-hosted-routing-engines-guide-2026/)
- **GraphHopper** — flexible "custom model" JSON profiles; **its truck profile and matrix code are closed-source/commercial**, [GitHub](https://github.com/gis-ops/tutorials/blob/master/general/foss_routing_engines_overview.md) though basic dimension constraints are in the open core. [Geospatialrouting](https://www.geospatialrouting.com/routing-api-automation-fleet-integration/comparing-routing-engines-for-production/)

**Recommendation:** For offline truck routing, either license the Mapbox Navigation SDK (fast to ship, but no true truck profile) or self-host **Valhalla** with a truck costing model (more work, but real truck/hazmat logic and no per-route fees).

### 6. Canadian truck-route / restriction data sources
- **Alberta 511** (511.alberta.ca) offers a **public REST API v3** (road conditions, cameras, ferries, events, alerts, weather) [511 Alberta](https://511.alberta.ca/developers/doc) — e.g., `https://511.alberta.ca/api/v3/get/winterroads`. [511 Alberta](https://511.alberta.ca/help/endpoint/winterroads) Good for closures/conditions; not a truck-restriction feed per se.
- **Alberta road bans**: the authoritative "Road ban list" is published on the Open Government portal (`open.alberta.ca/dataset/road-ban-list`, [Open Alberta](https://open.alberta.ca/dataset/road-ban-list) updated seasonally, most recent resource January 2026) and as a PDF from Transportation & Economic Corridors; toll-free line 1-855-ROADBAN. [Wcspermits](https://wcspermits.com/alberta-spring-road-bans/) Spring/Period-1 weights are triggered by thaw-depth readings (≥25 cm) from 70 frost-probe stations [Government of Alberta](https://www.alberta.ca/road-restrictions-and-bans-overview) — so timing is dynamic. Local/municipal road bans must be obtained from each county/MD individually. [Wcspermits](https://wcspermits.com/alberta-spring-road-bans/)
- **City of Calgary**: Truck Route Bylaw 60M90, Traffic Bylaw 26M96, and Transportation of Dangerous Goods Bylaw 13M2004 (dangerous goods restricted in the Central Business District 06:00–18:00 Mon–Sat, with carrier windows 09:30–11:30 and 13:30–15:30). Truck route map published by the city; fines up to $2,500. [City of Calgary](https://www.calgary.ca/bylaws/truck-routes.html)
- **City of Edmonton**: Traffic Bylaw 5590 designates truck routes and dangerous-goods routes; heavy vehicles (>8,000 kg GVW / 12.5 m) and placarded dangerous goods must stay on the Truck Route Network; [City of Edmonton](https://www.gov.edmonton.ab.ca/transportation/driving_carpooling/truck-routes) Low Level Bridge northbound posted at 28t/44t/50t for CS1/CS2/CS3; [City of Edmonton](https://www.gov.edmonton.ab.ca/transportation/driving_carpooling/truck-routes) spring road ban March 1–June 1. [City of Edmonton](https://www.edmonton.ca/transportation/truck-routes)
- **British Columbia**: **Open511-DriveBC API** provides real-time road events (closures, planned work, extreme weather) under the Open511 standard, licensed Open Government Licence – BC. [Open Government Portal](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/23a839e3-8fb4-4569-bb3d-c28a7621f687)
- **Alberta guidance**: "Guidelines for the Establishment of Dangerous Goods Routes in Alberta Municipalities" (Alberta Transportation) is on the open portal.

Most of these are **PDFs, bylaws, or condition feeds — not routable geometry** — so you will need to geocode/digitize dangerous-goods routes and encode road bans as time-boxed edge restrictions in your routing layer.

## Details / Integration architecture recommendation
A compliant, defensible LeaseOS stack:
1. **Location input**: LSD/NTS/UWI → coordinate via official polygon files (ATS v4.1 for AB [free], BCER DLS grid + BCGS for BC [free], licensed SaskGrid for SK [paid]). Snap to polygon centroid/corner; never use a bare formula.
2. **Map rendering**: MapLibre + your own vector tiles (Overture/OSM) or Mapbox — keeps you clear of Google/Apple map-pairing restrictions and works offline.
3. **Routing**: HERE Routing v8 (truck + hazmat) online; Valhalla truck costing or Mapbox Navigation SDK offline. Overlay Alberta/Sask road-ban and municipal DG-route restrictions yourself.
4. **POI/business search**: HERE Places, Mapbox Search, or Foursquare — NOT Google Places or Apple MapKit alongside a non-native map.
5. **Live conditions**: Alberta 511 API, DriveBC Open511, Alberta road-ban list.

## Recommendations
1. **Do not architect around Google Places or Apple MapKit.** The "No use with a non-Google map" clause and MapKit's explicit fleet-management prohibition make them non-viable as the POI layer for a mixed stack. Choose HERE Places or Foursquare on day one. *Threshold to revisit*: only if you render the actual Google map and route with Google — but Google LVR is contiguous-US/Japan only, so this fails for Western Canada today.
2. **Start with HERE Routing v8 for truck + TDG routing.** It is the only mainstream API that (a) has real Canadian commercial-vehicle attribution, (b) supports hazmat parameters, and (c) bills truck routing at car-route rates. Prototype on the free ~250k-transaction tier. *Threshold to switch to Trimble PC*MILER*: if freight-billing-grade mileage accuracy or legal-standard freight contracts become a requirement.
3. **License Alberta ATS v4.1 (free) and BC grids (free) immediately; budget for Saskatchewan SaskGrid** (~$200–305/yr last published in 2017; confirm current price with ISC). Build your converter as a spatial lookup against these files, not a formula.
4. **For offline, pilot Valhalla with a truck costing model.** If in-house routing-engine ops are too heavy, fall back to the Mapbox Navigation SDK offline (accepting it lacks a true truck profile) and layer your own restriction checks.
5. **Build a road-ban / dangerous-goods overlay service** that ingests the Alberta road-ban list, Alberta 511, DriveBC, and municipal bylaws (Calgary 13M2004, Edmonton 5590), digitizes DG routes, and applies them as time-boxed restrictions on top of the base truck route. This is your genuine differentiator versus Lease Locator / OilTrails.
6. **Confirm truck-routing coverage tiers for Western Canada rural roads** with both HERE and Trimble sales before committing — request a trial key and test against real lease roads, not just highways.

## Caveats
- **Pricing is volatile and partly quote-based.** HERE list prices rose ~6% on April 1, 2026; Trimble API pricing is by quote; Foursquare changed pricing June 1, 2026; the Saskatchewan SaskGrid figure ($202.91/$304.36 per year) is from a 2017 fee table and is not confirmed for 2026. Treat all dollar figures as directional and confirm with vendors.
- **Google LVR could expand to Canada** after its August 2026 contiguous-US/Japan launch — monitor, but do not plan around it yet.
- **Commercial APIs generally do NOT encode Canadian seasonal road bans or municipal DG routes**; you must source and maintain these separately.
- **Rural/backroad and lease-road coverage is weak across all providers**; satellite imagery + user-contributed pins remain necessary, as the incumbents demonstrate.
- **Verify Manitoba DLS data licensing** separately if you expand east; it was not confirmed in this research.
- **ToS change over time.** The Google/Apple clauses quoted are current as of the versions reviewed; re-verify the live terms before launch, especially the Places UI Kit exception and MapKit's fleet-management prohibition.
