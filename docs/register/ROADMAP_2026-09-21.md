## Security, CI and ops

- One-year JWTs with no server-side revocation.
- `SameSite=None` with no explicit CSRF policy.
- Uploads trust the client's MIME type.
- No SAST, dependency or secret scanning in CI.
- No browser or native E2E suite.
- Health endpoint always returns `ok: true`.
- Thin observability; no deploy, backup or restore runbooks.
- No check for duplicate migration prefixes — there are two `0157`s.
