LeaseOS Specialized Freight & Movement
 Compliance
 Build Plan v1 - highway, specialty freight, over-dimensional movement, escort-provider integration, secure custody
 and evidence workflows
Design principle: LeaseOS should determine the applicable rule packs from the vehicle, cargo, route, jurisdiction and job
- then fail closed when a legally or operationally required condition is unknown, expired, missing or contradicted.
Third-party service providers remain interchangeable and are not hard-coded to any named company.
1. Core Architecture
Component
Build requirement
Specialty profile registry
Versioned rule packs activated by cargo, vehicle, movement type, jurisdiction and permit conditions.
Stackable classifications
A trip can simultaneously be TDG + tanker + overweight + overheight + secure-custody, rather than forcing one category.
Pre-departure gate
Evaluate driver, unit, trailer, cargo, documents, permits, route, weather/road restrictions, securement and required support services.
Authority hierarchy
Permit-specific conditions and current authoritative rules override generic LeaseOS guidance. Store source, effective dates and verification status.
Evidence ledger
Timestamped actor, GPS, document/version, photos where appropriate, signatures, inspection events, corrections and reason codes.
Exception engine
Missing/expired/unknown/contradictory items become visible exceptions; safety-critical exceptions block dispatch.
2. Specialty Freight Rule Packs
Fuel / petroleum tanker
TDG classification and shipping document linkage; tank/trailer identity and inspection status; placard validation;
loading/unloading checklist; grounding/bonding or site-required controls; compartment/load record; spill/emergency workflow;
seal and custody events where used.
Propane, compressed gas and cryogenic
Pressure-vessel/trailer documentation; commodity-specific TDG controls; fill/load records; unloading connection checklist;
emergency information; temperature/pressure telemetry interfaces where equipment supports them.
Refrigerated / temperature-controlled
Cargo-specific setpoint; pre-cool; continuous temperature history; door events; defrost events; sensor calibration status;
sanitation records; temperature excursion workflow; receiver acceptance/rejection and cold-chain evidence report.
Dangerous goods general
UN number, shipping name, class/division, subsidiary class, packing group where applicable, quantity/container type, shipping
document, placards/marks, training credential status, emergency-assistance requirements and jurisdictional validation.
Explosives / Class 1
Dedicated high-security compliance profile. Validate applicable authorization, classification/compatibility information,
documentation, custody, authorized receiver and other legally required movement conditions. Keep sensitive operational
information role-restricted.


Firearms / controlled or high-value freight
Commercial freight custody profile: authorized parties, package/pallet counts, seal/tamper events where used, receiver
verification, proof of delivery and strict role-based visibility. Ammunition is separately evaluated for applicable TDG
requirements.
Radioactive / regulated sources
Dedicated regulated-material rule pack with authority-specific documentation, package/category fields, trained personnel
validation, custody and emergency-response information. No generic rule should substitute for the applicable regulator.
Lithium batteries / EV battery packs
Battery chemistry/type and condition, packaging status, damaged/defective exception, TDG classification where applicable,
temperature/fire exception workflow and carrier documentation.
Waste / vacuum / oilfield fluids
Waste stream classification, generator, origin, quantity, manifest/ticket, approved destination, load/unload custody, partial
acceptance, rejection, disposal receipt and complete generator-to-facility chain of custody.
Livestock
Species/load data, welfare/rest/water/ventilation requirements by jurisdiction, condition checks, delays, transfer-of-care records
and exception escalation.
Logging / pipe / coils / precast / machinery
Commodity-specific securement templates, geometry/weight, blocking/bracing, tiedown/WLL evidence, edge protection,
load-shift inspections and route constraints.
High-value / sensitive cargo
Need-to-know manifest visibility, custody scans, tamper exceptions, authorized receiver, proof of delivery and audit trail without
exposing sensitive detail on ordinary dispatch/map views.


3. Heavy & Over-Dimensional Movement Engine
Movement dimension
LeaseOS planning fields / gates
Overwidth
Loaded width, permit thresholds/conditions, lane/route constraints, escort requirements, signs/lights/flags as required by authority.
Overheight / high load
Loaded height, verified clearance data, unknown-clearance block, overhead utility/signal/structure conflicts, high-load support workflow.
Overlength / overhang
Overall combination length, load length, front/rear projection, turning constraints, permit/escort conditions.
Overweight
GVW plus individual axle and axle-group loads; permit limits; road/bridge/seasonal restrictions; route-specific evaluation.
Special configurations
Mobile cranes, service rigs, long combination vehicles, multi-axle heavy haul, steering dollies/boosters/jeeps and other configurable axle groups.
Travel restrictions
Permit time windows, daylight/night restrictions, weather/visibility conditions, road bans, construction, municipal restrictions and jurisdiction boundaries.
4. Cargo Securement Engine
Securement should be a reusable calculation-and-evidence subsystem, not a generic checkbox. Capture cargo type, pieces,
mass, dimensions, center-of-gravity information when known, anchor points, tiedown type/count, working load limits,
blocking/bracing, dunnage, friction/anti-slip material and edge protection. Commodity-specific rules remain authoritative.
Workflow
Implementation
Plan
Generate a securement plan from cargo + vehicle + applicable commodity rule pack.
Verify
Driver confirms actual tiedowns/anchors and records exceptions; required evidence can be attached.
Reinspect
Rule engine schedules required en-route inspections based on the applicable jurisdiction/rules rather than a hard-coded universal distance.
Exception
Shift, damaged tiedown, broken seal, compromised packaging or changed load geometry opens a corrective workflow.
Audit
Preserve original plan, inspection events, corrections, actor and reason instead of silently overwriting.
5. Pilot / Escort Vehicle Program
LeaseOS does not need to supply escort services. Build the operational parameters and an open third-party provider interface.
No provider name is assumed or embedded.
 Determine escort requirement from permit conditions, movement dimensions, route and jurisdiction; store front/rear positioning
and any additional authority-directed support.
 Provider qualification object: service region, credentials/qualifications where applicable, insurance, equipment capabilities,
availability and verification dates.
 Assignment package: permit, route/version, dimensions, movement window, communication plan, meeting point and
authorized operational contacts - only the information the provider needs.
 Trip state: requested -> accepted -> mobilizing -> staged -> active -> complete -> disputed/cancelled, with timestamps and
evidence.
 Communication check before movement and event reporting for route blockage, clearance concern, breakdown, weather
deterioration or permit-condition conflict.
 Provider cannot edit LeaseOS regulatory rules. Field feedback becomes a review request handled through the
compliance-content change process.
 Service-provider billing remains separate from LeaseOS subscription/platform billing; support configurable
referral/platform/service-management fee models later.
6. Third-Party Compliance / Field Validation Partner
Create an unnamed, non-exclusive Service Partner role. A future trucking-safety, pilot/escort or compliance company can audit
workflows, perform field validation or deliver contracted services without becoming the authority that writes LeaseOS rules.


Government/permit sources remain the compliance authority; partner findings enter a controlled review and approval workflow.

8. Dispatch Gate Sequence
1. Resolve tenant, job, driver, unit, trailers and actual cargo.
2. Classify applicable specialty profiles; allow multiple simultaneous profiles.
3. Resolve jurisdiction(s), route, permit conditions and current versioned rule sources.
4. Validate driver/unit/trailer credentials and inspections.
5. Validate cargo documentation, TDG/regulated-material requirements and custody requirements where applicable.
6. Evaluate dimensions, axle groups, road/bridge/clearance constraints and permit conditions.
7. Validate securement plan and required inspection schedule.
8. Determine required escort/pilot/utility/police or other support from authoritative conditions.
9. Evaluate road/weather/time-window restrictions and offline-data freshness.
10. Return READY, READY_WITH_ACKNOWLEDGED_NONSAFETY_EXCEPTIONS, or BLOCKED. Safety-critical unknowns
are never treated as safe.
11. At departure, freeze a dependency fingerprint of the rule versions, permit, route and evidence used for the decision.

10. Important Product Boundaries
 LeaseOS should not claim that a calculated route, securement plan or permit interpretation is itself legal authorization. The
applicable authority and issued permit remain controlling.
 Do not hard-code example temperatures, escort thresholds, securement distances, dimensions or weights as universal legal
rules. They vary by cargo, equipment, jurisdiction, permit and rule version.
 Sensitive cargo information should use least-privilege access, encrypted storage/transport, immutable audit events and
minimized map/dispatch display.
 Regulatory data needs source provenance, effective dates, review dates and a stale-data state. Unknown or stale
safety-critical data cannot silently become 'clear'.
 Third-party validation is valuable but does not replace required regulatory, legal, engineering or authority approval.
11. Relationship to Existing LeaseOS Plan
This package extends the existing LeaseOS mapping/routing, provenance, confirmation, audit, offline-first and dispatch-gate
architecture. It should be implemented after the core stabilization and tenant-scoping work, then integrated into the same
canonical gateway rather than built as a parallel application.
