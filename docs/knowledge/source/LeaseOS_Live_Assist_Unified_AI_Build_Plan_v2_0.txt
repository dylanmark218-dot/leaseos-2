Recommended delivery strategy
Do not start with whole-device control. First build the authority gateway, typed action registry and unified AI 
Secretary. Then add read-only Live Assist. Only after the evidence, confirmation and policy gates are proven should 
LeaseOS enable deterministic cross-app actions.
1. Executive build brief
LeaseOS Live Assist is not a separate chatbot and not a generic remote-control bot. It is the visual/voice 
interaction layer for one unified LeaseOS Assistant. The user talks to one assistant; behind the scenes, an 
orchestrator routes work to approved capabilities for paperwork, driving, routing, dispatch, compliance, billing, 
training, mechanics and company operations.
The build must preserve a hard separation between AI reasoning and authoritative system writes. AI can 
observe, explain, extract, recommend and prepare. Actual business changes pass through a typed Action 
Gateway that checks tenant, identity, role, device trust, job context, vehicle state, risk, evidence and 
confirmation requirements before executing.

Primary outcome
A driver, mechanic, dispatcher or office user should be able to say what they need in ordinary language, optionally 
share the Android screen, and receive help that is grounded in LeaseOS records and approved sources - without 
giving an LLM direct database write access or unrestricted device control.
Success criteria
 One visible LeaseOS Assistant; specialist routing is internal.
 Works on personal/BYOD Android and company-managed fleet Android devices.
 Hybrid local-first processing: wake phrase, basic OCR/UI understanding and simple commands stay local 
when practical; cloud models are used only when needed.
 Parked / moving / emergency modes materially change allowed actions, not merely the UI.
 Important answers show source, freshness, confidence and whether content is measured, observed, 
historical, inferred or confirmed.
 No consequential action is committed without the required authority and confirmation.
 Offline operation degrades safely: cached data remains usable, stale/live dependencies are labeled, writes 
queue for synchronization.
 Raw screen/audio is transient by default; durable business facts are stored as structured records with 
provenance.
 Mechanics assistance can diagnose and prepare work, but cannot autonomously declare a vehicle safe or 
release it to service.

5.3 Explainability
Operationally meaningful answers should support a “Why?” view. The answer payload should carry source 
class, source identifier, observed/verified time, freshness, confidence and the boundary between evidence and 
model inference.
Example diagnostic statement
MEASURED   coolant_temp = 112 C      source: ECU sensor
OBSERVED   residue near upper hose   source: mechanic photo
HISTORICAL similar loss 18 days ago  source: WO-1842
INFERRED   probable cooling leak      source: assistant reasoning
CONFIRMED  upper radiator hose leak   verified_by: mechanic
6. Android Live Assist architecture
6.1 BYOD/personal devices
 Use Android MediaProjection for explicit whole-screen capture sessions. User consent is requested for each 
capture session where required by Android behavior.
 Show a persistent LeaseOS “LIVE ASSIST ACTIVE” control plus Android/system capture indication. Either 
stop path terminates capture and releases resources.
 Process frame fingerprints, UI semantics and OCR locally first; do not continuously stream video to the 
cloud.
 Redact sensitive regions when possible before cloud escalation. Treat password/PIN/biometric/secure 
screens as user-only steps.
 Use official APIs, LeaseOS APIs, Android intents and deep links before any accessibility-based automation.
 If AccessibilityService is used for automation, constrain it to declared, narrow, deterministic workflows. Do 
not give an LLM an unrestricted plan-and-tap loop.
6.2 Company-managed fleet devices
 Use Android Enterprise/MDM policy to standardize allowed apps, security posture, data-sharing policy and 
Live Assist availability.
 Respect device-owner/profile-owner restrictions that disable content capture or AI/agent automation.
 Keep company policy separate from the non-bypassable LeaseOS safety floor: MDM may reduce capability, 
never silently expand assistant authority.
 Support separate profiles/policies for driver tablets, shop/mechanic devices and office/dispatch devices.

6.4 Android/Google Play implementation constraints
Current platform constraint - re-verify before store submission
Google Play policy permits narrow, clearly understood deterministic accessibility automation, but prohibits using 
AccessibilityService so an app autonomously initiates, plans and executes actions or decisions. Build LeaseOS 
around typed actions, intents/APIs and deterministic adapters - not unrestricted AI UI control.

10. Test strategy and production gates
10.1 Required automated test families
 Authorization matrix: every action x role x tenant x driving state x risk level.
 Tenant isolation: retrieval, embeddings, screen observations, task queues and audit refs.
 Proposal/confirmation: uncertain OCR/voice values never silently become confirmed records.
 Capture lifecycle: deny, start, rotate, app switch, protected content, revoke, process death, reboot.
 Moving-mode enforcement: prohibited UI/action pathways remain unavailable even if requested by model 
output.
 Offline/stale handling: cached data labels, queued writes, retries, conflict resolution and no false 
verification.
 Action idempotency/replay: duplicate model/tool calls cannot duplicate invoices, tickets, messages or work 
orders.
 Model failure/hallucination: invalid/unknown action or argument is rejected before executor.
 Approval step-up: L4 action cannot execute with missing/expired/wrong-role approval.
 Managed policy: content-capture/AI restrictions are detected and honored.
 Retention/redaction: transient screen frames/audio are removed according to policy and sensitive values 
are not leaked to logs.
 Accessibility and one-handed/mobile: large text, screen reader, gloves/large touch targets where supported, 
voice fallback.

11. Security, privacy and platform compliance
11.1 Threat model priorities
Threat Mitigation
Prompt injection from screen/doc Treat external content as untrusted data. It cannot redefine 
assistant policy or authorize actions.
Cross-tenant retrieval Tenant-scoped indexes/queries and policy-enforced joins; tests 
for every retrieval path.
Credential capture
Detect/redact sensitive UI where possible; never persist 
passwords/PINs/tokens from capture; secure screens remain 
user-only.
Over-broad screen retention Frame TTL and structured-observation model; evidence 
retention must be explicit and job/policy linked.
Unauthorized external action Typed allowlist + risk/role/context checks + idempotency + 
confirmation.
Model tool abuse Model can request only known capabilities; gateway validates 
all arguments server-side.
Lost/stolen device Device binding, secure local storage, session timeout, 
revocation, managed wipe where applicable.
Audit tampering Append-only audit trail with integrity controls and durable 
references to evidence/action receipts.

18.1 Mandatory cross-cutting tests
 Tenant isolation across operational records, search indexes, evidence artifacts, task queues and audit 
receipts.
 Role/temporary-delegation matrix for every typed action and capability.
 Parked/moving/emergency state enforcement at gateway level even when a model requests a prohibited 
action.
 Unknown/stale/conflicting source behavior; unknown is never converted to safe/clear/verified.
 Field-level precision and provenance for voice/OCR; approximate values stay approximate until confirmed.
 Mechanic release and dispatch readiness cannot be bypassed by AI recommendations.
 Idempotency/replay protection for work orders, invoices, ticket submission, messages and external actions.
 Optimistic concurrency and transactional conflict handling for simultaneous office/field updates.
 Prompt-injection tests against uploaded manuals, PDFs, web snapshots and OCR text.
 Live Assist capture lifecycle: denied, started, backgrounded, rotated, revoked, protected screen, process 
death and reboot.
 Retention and redaction: transient screen/audio/OCR intermediates expire and secrets are absent from audit 
logs.
 Source/version reproducibility: important verdicts can show which source revision and evidence were used.
18.2 Production-ready definition for this subsystem set
 The Assistant can explain and prepare work without bypassing existing dispatch, HOS, routing, billing, 
signature or mechanic-release authority boundaries.
 Every consequential workflow has a typed plan, explicit evidence, confirmation record where required, and 
an AssistantReceipt.
 Knowledge retrieval is tenant/role/customer/equipment scoped before search and can identify stale, 
superseded, conflicting or unverified sources.
 Evidence ingestion preserves originals, field-level claims and amendment history; 
duplicate/wrong-job/conflicting evidence is surfaced instead of silently merged.
 Offline behavior degrades safely and synchronizes without duplicating or overwriting newer authoritative 
state.
 Live Assist is visibly active, revocable, transient by default, and incapable of turning general AI reasoning 
into unrestricted device control.
