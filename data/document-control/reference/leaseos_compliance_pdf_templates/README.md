# LeaseOS — Compliance & Permits PDF Templates

Generated as blank, printable templates for the LeaseOS document engine.

## Included
1. DOT / FMCSA Registration & Authority Record
2. Oilfield Waste Tracking / Generator Compliance Form
3. NORM Survey & Handling Record
4. Environmental Compliance / Spill Reporting Form

These are internal record templates, not official government forms. The application should store jurisdiction, applicability, source, verification date, evidence attachments, reviewer, and renewal/next-review metadata.

Official reference points used while designing the fields:
- FMCSA registration and authority requirements: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/registration-forms
- FMCSA operating authority: https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/get-mc-number-authority-operate
- EPA hazardous-waste manifest system: https://www.epa.gov/hwgenerators/hazardous-waste-manifest-system

Do not represent these PDFs as official FMCSA/EPA forms. For regulated submissions, use the current government form or electronic system required by the applicable jurisdiction.
